    Platform

    What they're fighting for

    Each street in Capitol Crashers is one fight from the real platforms. Win the street and the policy passes on the block. Planks shortened from their campaign sites, sourced in the comic's back matter.

    Street01

    Freeze the Rent

    The block · Mamdani

    • Freeze the rent for every stabilized tenant. More than two million New Yorkers live in those apartments.
    • 200,000 permanently affordable, union-built, rent-stabilized homes over ten years. Fully affordable projects get fast-tracked.
    • One office for code enforcement. If the owner won't make the repair, the city does and sends the bill. In the worst cases, the city takes the building.
    Boss · The Landlord
    Street02

    Healthcare for All

    The clinic block · El-Sayed

    • Medicare for every American, cradle to grave, with vision, dental and hearing. No premiums, copays or deductibles.
    • Medical debt abolished.
    • At least 50 gallons of clean water a person a day, bills capped at 2% of income, every lead pipe in Michigan out by 2030.
    Boss · The Insurance Exec
    Street03

    Money Out of Politics

    The cable studio · El-Sayed

    • Never taken a dime of corporate money, never will. Overturn Citizens United.
    • Ban outside spending by corporate 501(c)(4)s, super PACs and 527s. Public election funding and spending caps.
    • Tax capital gains over $1 million like wages, tax wealth over $1 billion, exempt the first $50,000. One good job should raise a family.
    Boss · The Lobbyist
    Street04

    Accountability

    The Capitol approach · both

    • Witnesses with receipts walk the street with you. Keep them standing and the record gets read.
    • Who pays: match New Jersey's 11.5% corporate tax, a 2% city tax on income over $1 million, fines on corrupt landlords.
    • Free childcare from six weeks to five, a $30 minimum wage by 2030, fare-free buses, city-owned groceries.
