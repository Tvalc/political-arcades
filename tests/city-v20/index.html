How your neighborhood grows.

Give your next run a place to go. You choose what your neighbors get to enjoy.

    01

    Bring something home.

    Play any cabinet. Earn its supplies, then return to your city with a clear reward receipt.

  2. Supplies → places
    02

    Put it on your block.

    Pick a court, garden, home or care space. Choose its plot. Your stockpile shows exactly what it costs.

  3. Visits → community effort
    03

    Make it a gathering place.

    Neighbors visit, practice and make friends. Their effort funds upgrades; regulars grow into mentors.

    04

    Share the good things.

    Bring a league to opening day or deliver a garden or care project. Unlock another block and choose what grows next.
