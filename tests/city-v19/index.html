How your neighborhood grows

Choose open pickup or a youth team. Build a court with VOTE supplies, or redesign a plot and reclaim its original supplies. Households sign up by practicing. A returning player becomes a volunteer coach, while family visits to homes, gardens and kitchens recruit opening-day volunteers. The organizer can help recruit at an assigned place.

When the team is ready, start opening day. Neighbors walk to the court and play together. Completing the league unlocks another block. Kitchens can host distinct programs. All improvements are positive; these residents and their league are fictional. Real-world giving is reported separately.
