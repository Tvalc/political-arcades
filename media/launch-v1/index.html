AdsToAid is live: five free arcade games, one neighborhood of your own.

Shoot hoops in VOTE. Clear rows in Housing Stack. Repair streets in Pothole Party. Play Every Kid Can See or Capitol Crashers. Each game earns supplies you can spend on your city.

If the site gives less than $50 in a month, I personally cover the difference so total monthly giving reaches $50. My top-up goes to a local Seattle cause. This is a commitment, not a claim that donations have already been delivered.
