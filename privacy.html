Privacy

Political Arcades games run in your browser. They do not ask for an account, and they do not send your score or controls to a server.

If a display ad is shown on a game page, that ad is served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to measure the ad and to choose which ad to show. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The Patreon link leaves this site. Patreon handles any payment on its own pages.
