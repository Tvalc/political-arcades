Your choices

Privacy

Play without an account. Keep saves on your device, or choose an optional backup. Analytics and loading external video players are separate choices.

Updated October 19, 2026 · Contact Tony

Saves on this device

AdsToAid / Political Arcades games store progress, scores, preferences and your fictional city in browser storage. Clearing site data or using another browser can remove access to that local progress. Playing does not create an account. Downloaded backup files stay wherever you save them.

Optional online backups and accounts

The city’s Save progress menu can create a guest cloud backup without a signup, restore a private recovery code, download or import a file, or connect an account through our save service. Online saving sends a copy of supported arcade records, supplies, city name, neighborhood state and preferences to that service. A connected backup checks for changes while the city page is open. You can continue playing locally without it.

Anyone with your private recovery code or recovery link can access that backup. Keep it private. “Use this device only” stops this browser’s connection; it does not erase the cloud copy. Contact Tony for a stored-data or deletion request. Do not email passwords, recovery codes or sign-in tokens. We will arrange how to identify the relevant save.

Account sign-in is handled by the save service and its identity providers. We do not receive your Google or ChatGPT password. The account service receives the identity information returned by the sign-in provider, such as an account identifier and email address. Supabase supports the Google/email account connection. See Supabase privacy, Google privacy and OpenAI privacy.

Public showcase links

Sharing a showcase is optional. A public link contains a snapshot of the city and selected records, including the display name you choose. Anyone with the link can view or forward it. It is different from a private recovery link and does not give editing access to a save. Avoid putting personal information in a public city or player name.

Optional analytics

If you allow analytics, PostHog measures page visits, game starts, city resources earned, city builds, return visits and Patreon-link clicks. A browser identifier connects visits. We do not send city names, form contents or your chosen political character. Session recording and automatic click capture are disabled. Recorded URLs exclude query strings and fragments. Game resources do not measure donations or revenue.

Declining does not affect the games. We honor browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can change your choice below. This choice does not load or authorize external video players.

Official video players

Greatest Hits displays our thumbnails and editorial notes first. YouTube and TikTok players load only when you choose “Load player.” Loading connects your browser to that provider, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address, device/browser information and the referring site. Its own storage, playback and privacy practices then apply. YouTube embeds use its privacy-enhanced domain; that is not a promise of anonymous playback.

You can use “Watch original upload” instead, or close the player. Closing it or choosing another clip removes the embedded frame. See Google / YouTube privacy and TikTok privacy. Our original music videos and game media are separate productions and may be delivered from our connected hosting domains.

Advertising

This site currently shows clearly labeled house promotions for Makko, which Tony owns. Those links go to an external site. We have paused automatic Google ad loading on PoliticalArcades.com while AdSense approval and the advertising consent configuration are being completed. Our AdSense ownership-verification metadata and ads.txt seller entry remain public; they are not proof of approval.

If Google advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may place or read cookies, use web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers, and process data to deliver, personalize and measure ads. Any required advertising consent controls must be in place before that loading is enabled. The analytics choice above is not an advertising consent manager. Learn how Google uses data on partner sites, manage personalized Google ads through My Ad Center, or see YourAdChoices.

Connected pages and services

Capitol Crashers is hosted on capitolcrashers.com and embedded inside the arcade. Its checked browser messages can transfer game rewards to your city; a link fragment can also carry a reward receipt. Hosting providers receive normal connection information when serving pages, images, games and audio. Some pages use Google Fonts. These requests are distinct from optional analytics.

The Dispatch signup opens a Brevo form. Playing does not subscribe you. Patreon handles optional support and payments on its own pages; this site does not collect payment-card details. See Brevo privacy and Patreon privacy. Games played on Playgama use that service’s privacy and advertising practices.

Audience and contact

The arcade is general-audience political satire and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us about a child’s submitted information or any privacy concern at tony@makko.ai. Game supplies and residents are fictional. Real giving is reported separately in the Community Pot.
