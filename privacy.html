Privacy

AdsToAid games run in your browser and do not require an account. Games and the optional city save progress and preferences in browser storage. Those saves are specific to this browser and site; clearing site data can remove them. Cross-device account saving is not available.

Game progress and connected pages

Capitol Crashers runs on capitolcrashers.com. Its game-reward events can be passed to the AdsToAid city through a checked browser message or a link fragment when you visit your city. The city is fictional game progress and is separate from real donations. Hosting and third-party services receive normal connection information when your browser requests their content.

Newsletter and optional support

The Dispatch signup opens a Brevo form. Information you choose to submit there is handled by that service for the newsletter. Playing does not subscribe you. Patreon handles memberships and payments on its own pages. The site does not collect payment-card details.

Optional analytics

If you allow analytics, PostHog measures page visits, game starts, city resources earned, city builds, return visits and Patreon-link clicks. It uses a browser identifier to connect visits. We do not send city names, form contents or your chosen political character. Session recording and automatic click capture are disabled. URLs are sent without query strings or fragments. Game resources are not real-world donations or revenue.

You can decline without affecting any game, or change your choice here. We honor browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. · PostHog privacy policy

Advertising and cookies

Some game pages load Google’s advertising code, and some games may show Google ads between rounds or optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for an in-game perk. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies to serve and measure ads based on your previous visits to this site or other websites. Google advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize ads using those visits.

You can manage personalized Google ads in My Ad Center and opt out of some other vendors' personalized advertising through YourAdChoices. These choices do not remove all advertising. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland see Google’s consent message before advertising cookies are used and can change that choice at any time from the message’s link; where a consent message is displayed elsewhere, you can use its controls the same way.

Children

These games are made for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Copies on other game sites

Some games are also published on Playgama. When you play there, Playgama’s own privacy policy and advertising apply instead of this one.

Learn about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's cookies.

External pages and embedded services have their own privacy practices. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts; gameplay, audio and comic assets may come from our connected sites. See Brevo’s privacy policy and Patreon’s privacy information.
