THE JOKE IS SOMETHING YOU DO

Make the numbers tell on him.

Professor Profit is a fictional pundit with a very expensive suit and some extremely selective arithmetic. You pull apart his claims, expose what he left out, then fling the corrected answer back at him.

01 / THE RICH GUY IN THE AVERAGE

Remove the CEO.

He says his workers average $100,000. Drag his $260,000 paycheck out of their calculation and see what they actually earn.

02 / READ THE VERY SMALL PRINT

Find what disappeared.

Add the hidden fees, count the unpaid hours, and put the charity’s missing marketing bill back in its total.

03 / RETURN TO SENDER

Fire the proof.

Pull back the gold answer ball and release. Land your correction, watch the pundit lose his composure, and get the punchline.

Sammy’s patient use of arithmetic to challenge an argument, discussed in his PBS News Hour interview, inspired this direction. His official biography also highlights his wordplay and background. Every pundit, corporate claim, scenario and line of game dialogue here is our fictional writing, not a claim about a real company or an authentic Sammy quotation. No cloned voice is used.
