THE ACTUAL BIT

The wordplay is the game.

Sammy’s joke starts with wanting to teach the Pythagorean theorem. Instead, he finds himself putting out A’s fire and B’s fire. The turn to a cease-fire gives a familiar math lesson a very different meaning.

In our arcade version, that becomes a literal chalkboard full of overheated equations. Move close, spray from a safe distance, and keep enough water to finish before the bell. Clear fires to charge a move that cools the whole board.

01 / KEEP YOUR DISTANCE

A cooler head.

Get close enough to aim, but stay out of the flames. Hold SPRAY and the hose targets the nearest fire.

02 / PICK YOUR ROUTE

Show your working.

Dodge the erasers, watch for new sparks, and make a trip to a blue tap before your water runs out.

03 / END THE ARGUMENT

Class dismissed.

Put out five fires to charge Cease-fire. Use it when the board gets crowded, then finish the remaining equations.

The premise comes from Sammy’s stand-up excerpt in Leila Fadel’s NPR interview, August 14, 2024. His joke addresses the war in Gaza. This prototype turns the wordplay into a fictional chalkboard challenge. The game mechanics and new lines are ours.
