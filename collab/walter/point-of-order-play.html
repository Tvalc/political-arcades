What is real?

The excerpts use the public-domain King James Version. Read the linked chapter for context. Romans 13:1 is an opening-sentence excerpt.

Davis School District retained the KJV Bible at all school levels on June 20, 2023, following an appeal. The real decision considered the book’s value as a whole. This is a historical example of availability, not a claim about every school or current policy.

What is invented?

This board, blanket ban, dialogue and vote are fictional satire. They do not depict Davis’s actual meeting or policy, or establish why a real district retained a book. The point is to challenge blanket rules and selective enforcement, not to demand that the Bible be banned.
