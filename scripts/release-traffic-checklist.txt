POLITICAL ARCADES: RELEASE AND SEARCH CHECKLIST

Primary destinations
- Original music: /watch/<song>/ (one stable URL per finished video).
- Games: /games/<game>/ for explanations; link onward to the playable game.
- Real clips: a sourced Greatest Hits page when there is useful original context.

Search targets (intent hypotheses, not measured keyword volumes)
- Political Arcades; free political browser games; political satire games.
- James Talarico country song; Jon Ossoff music video; Zohran Mamdani song.
- Specific speaker + policy/quote represented by a sourced clip page.
Do not create empty candidate pages or unrelated news bait.

For each release
1. Publish the finished video, a clear poster, descriptive title and source/credits.
2. Update VideoObject and video-sitemap.xml with the actual published media URL.
   uploadDate must come from publication history, not today's rebuild time.
3. Add human-checked captions/transcript when available; never substitute invented
   words or a song draft for the released performance.
4. Prepare three distinct short cuts: strongest joke, strongest real quote, and
   most visually striking payoff. Each should make sense without the long cut.
5. Link to the exact watch page, not the home page. Example:
   https://politicalarcades.com/watch/name-the-day/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=name_the_day&utm_content=phone_refusal
   Use youtube, instagram, tiktok or bluesky as appropriate. No personal data in tags.
6. Share only with the user's authorization. Pitch relevant creators/newsletters
   with a specific clip, original source and a reason their audience would care.
7. Offer the related game or next video on the destination page. Newsletter is optional.

Measurement
- Search Console: indexed URLs, video indexing, impressions, queries, clicks.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: sitemap acceptance, indexed URLs and crawl errors.
- Platform analytics: short retention, outbound clicks, long-video retention.
- Existing consent-based site analytics: game_started, game_finished, return_visit.
- UTM links are prepared for attribution; do not claim a working attribution funnel
  until campaign dimensions and video-start events are verified in the dashboard.
- Review after 7 and 28 days; compare releases, not tiny day-to-day search samples.
