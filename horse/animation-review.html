    How to play

    Make them copy you. Pick a spot and a shot. If you make it, your opponent must match both. Miss a copy and you earn a letter. Spell VOTE and you lose.

    Move: arrows / WASD, or the touch stick. Shoot: press Space or tap Aim, then press again when the needle reaches the gold zone.

    Add flair: 1 Spin · 2 Dunk (near the rim) · 3 Fade · 4 Hook. Choose the active trick again to return to a regular shot. Practice has no letters.

    Unlock courts: win three matches to unlock the next court. Wins are saved in this browser; practice does not count.

    Pause: Esc or Pause. Restart and change shooter from the pause menu.
