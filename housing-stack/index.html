Make room for more.

  1. Fit the floors. Move and rotate each falling piece. The outline shows where it will land.
  2. Fill a row. Complete a horizontal row with no gaps. It clears and earns 1 building supply.
  3. Build your city. Your supplies stay saved when the stack reaches the top. Spend them in My city.

On touchscreens, use the buttons below the board. You can also tap the board to rotate or swipe sideways to move. Use Drop when you’re ready to place a piece.
