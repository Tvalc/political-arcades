What is the game?

Every Kid Can See is a moving-crew game with a concrete task: coordinate outreach, leave providers time to work and return for the crew. Completed visits bring families and activities into the courtyard. The celebration is part of the result of playing, rather than a separate illustration at the end.

How to play

  1. Steer with the joystick, swipe controls, WASD or arrow keys. Space pauses the run. Gold marks a drop-off; green marks a finished stop ready for collection.
  2. Keep moving while the provider finishes. Each completed pickup grows the crew, so the next visit asks you to manage a longer route.
  3. The courtyard opens up as completed visits accumulate. Care supplies earned in the arcade can be spent in My city; these are fictional game resources.

A good first run

Use the time between a gold drop-off and a green pickup to set up your next safe approach. The festival becomes livelier as your completed visits add up.

The idea behind it

The chapter celebrates useful public-health work while keeping credit with the people who did it. The real-world inspiration is the 2024 Wayne County / Vision To Learn partnership. The campus, crew dialogue and route are fictional; qualified clinicians, not the playable politicians, perform eye care.

Made for this arcade

Created and directed by Tony for AdsToAid / Political Arcades, using AI-assisted development and character art and animation workflows. Game art is made with Makko; Tony owns Makko. This is independent cartoon satire, with no campaign authorization or endorsement.

Finishing a round can contribute supplies to your city. Game progress and real charitable giving are different systems. The Community Pot records the latter.

Something went wrong?

Report the game, device and what happened. If local saving is important to you, do not clear your browser data as a troubleshooting step.
