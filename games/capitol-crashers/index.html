What is the game?

Capitol Crashers turns political confrontation into a cartoon street brawler. You move through the stage, make space around your fighter and decide when to commit to an attack. The cast and dialogue are satire; the fights are fictional. This is a playable game made for this arcade, rather than a clip of somebody else playing.

How to play

  1. Use the movement controls to approach an opponent, then attack when you have room to recover. Standing inside a crowd makes it harder to avoid the next hit.
  2. Read the on-screen character and control selection before the first stage. Mobile players use the touch controls; keyboard players use the controls shown by the game.
  3. Boss encounters change the rhythm. Watch their wind-up and recovery rather than treating every enemy like the last one. Pause before changing tabs or handing the phone to somebody else.

A good first run

Start with one fighter and learn the reach of the basic attack. Moving out of a crowded lane is often more useful than repeatedly tapping attack.

The idea behind it

The joke is in the mismatch between a serious political argument and an exuberant cartoon fight. A boss reaction is a comic performance, not documentary footage or a claim that a real encounter occurred.

Made for this arcade

Finishing a round can contribute supplies to your city. Game progress and real charitable giving are different systems. The Community Pot records the latter.

Something went wrong?

Report the game, device and what happened. If local saving is important to you, do not clear your browser data as a troubleshooting step.
