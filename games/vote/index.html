Basketball / shot matching

VOTE

Make a shot. Make them match it.

Play VOTEAll game guides →
Free browser gameMobile + keyboardNo download

What is the game?

VOTE is a basketball challenge built around matching a shot. The setter chooses a spot and a shot style. A successful basket asks the other players to reproduce both. Miss the copy and you collect a letter. Spell VOTE and you are out; the last player left wins. It is a small, readable contest that rewards consistency as much as a spectacular first basket.

How to play

  1. Move with the touch stick, arrow keys or WASD. Shooting takes two presses: begin aiming, then release in the gold zone. Space is the keyboard shoot control.
  2. Choose Solo vs 3 CPU or Pass and Play. In Pass and Play, hand the device to the highlighted player each turn. Practice has no elimination letters.
  3. If everyone matches, the next player sets. If somebody misses, the setter goes again. Win three matches to unlock the next court; wins are saved in this browser.

A good first run

Practice a regular shot before adding a spin, fade, hook or near-rim dunk. Make the timing familiar before making the copy harder.

The idea behind it

The candidates are sharing a court instead of a debate stage. Their different ball-handling performances give the same rules a different personality; choosing a favorite is part of the fun.
