How the split works

GIVING POLICY

The split is 50/50 at the first milestone only. Tony’s share then falls to 40%, 25%, 20%, 15%, 10% and finally 1% of future distributable profit. See the seven milestones and what Tony’s share pays for. These rates apply to distributable profit from advertising, Patreon and any future merchandise sales. Personal milestone spending comes from Tony’s share, not project costs.

Why does Makko count too?

Tony owns Makko. The $50 art bill is included in the $200 operating costs above. We also count it when checking how much Tony and his business have received. It is one payment, counted in two checks—not two expenses.

Even after the community’s full $350 is paid, Tony and Makko together cannot receive more than $350. With $50 already paid to Makko, Tony’s direct payments are limited to $300. Any blocked owner share stays unpaid.

Reserve policy and cost controls

The approved reserve target is one month of operating costs, based on the trailing three-month average. Additions and releases will be shown separately. Reserve cash is not compensation. Gifted credits are noncash support, not a fictional cash expense. Existing production subscriptions still need their project-funded share confirmed.

The promises behind the numbers

Community input. Tony decides.

Tony plans to invite recipient recommendations from the featured politicians, and may choose aligned community causes if they do not respond. Their participation or endorsement is not assumed. Tony has final say; site ballots and member voting are not available yet.

Projects, not politicians.

Verified community efforts only. No campaigns or recipients from which recommending politicians could financially benefit.

A $50 monthly giving floor.

If the site gives less than $50 in a month, Tony personally covers the difference, with his top-up going to a local Seattle cause. $30 from the site means $20 from Tony; $0 means $50; $50 or more means no top-up. His personal gift is not a project expense and never increases his payout limit.

Receipts, not promises.

Actual payments need redacted evidence. Allocations and estimates stay clearly labeled.

Community participation

Optional accounts save game progress. They do not provide recipient voting. There is no active ballot, spending-weighted vote or nomination calendar. Any future participation system will be explained before it opens. The external newsletter signup is optional and separate from playing.

Reporting, corrections and owner compensation
