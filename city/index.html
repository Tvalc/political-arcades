How your neighborhood grows

Select an open plot to build with supplies earned in the arcade. Families choose places to visit and complete activities while this city is open. Each completed activity earns one community spirit. Spend spirit to open a daily program, then turn a place into a community anchor. Better programs welcome more families and finish activities faster.

Prioritize a place to attract visitors. Send the organizer or El-Sayed to help its team; the care team gets a bigger boost with El-Sayed. All improvements are positive. Your residents, buildings and activities are fictional game stories. Real-world giving is reported separately.

City activity pauses when you leave the tab. Buildings, programs, identities and completed visits are saved in this browser.
