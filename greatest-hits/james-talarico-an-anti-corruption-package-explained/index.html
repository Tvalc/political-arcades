Why we selected it

A package of anti-corruption proposals is more testable than a promise to clean up politics. The useful part is the connection between a named practice and a rule meant to change it: follow those connections rather than the umbrella label.

What to listen for

Separate each proposed restriction and ask who would enforce it. Announcing a package does not mean its provisions have passed or would operate identically.
