Published 2025-12-25 by Zohran Mamdani for NYC.

Publisher's title: Most New Yorkers Can't Afford Childcare

Why we selected it

The childcare argument begins with a household expense rather than an abstract program name. That choice makes affordability concrete and invites a second question: what arrangement would move the cost, reduce it or make care easier to obtain?

What to listen for

This is a campaign-era case. Distinguish the ambition from eligibility, available places, staffing and funding; the short upload does not establish that a service has already opened.
