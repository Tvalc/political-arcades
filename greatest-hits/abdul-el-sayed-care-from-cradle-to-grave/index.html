Published 2026-10-09 by Abdul El-Sayed.

Publisher's title: Pass Medicare for All. Cradle to grave, it's there for you.

Why we selected it

The cradle-to-grave framing makes continuity the point: care should not disappear when a job, age or family situation changes. We chose it because it turns a large program name into a recognizable need over a lifetime.

What to listen for

Listen for how the promise would change eligibility and access. The short argument alone does not establish the proposed benefits, financing or transition rules.
