A game is not working

Tell us the game, your phone or computer, browser and the action just before the problem. A page URL and a screenshot are helpful. Never send passwords, account recovery codes or a private save link.

A source or credit needs correcting

Send the page, the specific sentence or clip title and a link to supporting evidence. For a rights or permissions concern, identify the work and the upload or page in question so it can be reviewed.
