THE FLOOR’S NEWEST MACHINE

HOW IT PLAYS

Less talk.
More asphalt.

  1. Drop off. Collect the marked supplies, then cross the marked job to deliver supplies and leave a worker. Keep the line moving.
  2. Give them a minute. Well, 4.2 seconds. The timer counts down while they work. A finished job earns 1 street work for your city and restores its part of the map.
  3. Circle back. Green means ready. Collect the worker and a neighbor joins the crew. Pick them up early and the job stays unfinished. Keep repairing until you crash. Every 15 completed jobs unlocks the next neighborhood. Collect the remaining workers to travel; your resources and crew carry over.
Controls & crew tips

Use arrows or WASD, swipe on the street, or tap or slide on the D-pad. Space pauses. Avoid the edge and your own line. A lone leader must collect workers before dropping off anyone else. Timers stop on a break.

All three heroes join every shift. Select a leader before starting; selections during a shift apply to your next shift. Your best score and unlocked neighborhoods save in this browser. After Texas, the run continues into another round of neighborhoods.
