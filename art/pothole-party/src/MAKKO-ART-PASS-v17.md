# Pothole Party Makko art pass — v17

All new illustrated art in this release was generated and saved through Makko Art Studio in Alley Relay / Arcade Site. No imagegen tool was used for this pass.

## Runtime assets
- materials-v17.webp: twelve distinct construction material bundles.
- construction-v17.webp: five construction categories with three illustrated stages each.
- weathered-v17.webp: six worn surface textures.
- residents-v17.webp: four-frame basketball, gardening, and schoolchild groups.
- work-crew-v17.webp: four-frame hammer, watering, and wrench loops for Mamdani, El-Sayed, Talarico, and the volunteer; one identity reference per generated sheet.

The first combined crew sheets mixed faces in some frames and were rejected. The individual retries are the shipped artwork. All actions for a given character share one scale. Packing translates frames to a common feet baseline without stretching or fitting each frame independently. The approved walk, dig, and pothole art remain in use for paving work.

## Restoration
The clean NYC, Detroit, and San Antonio maps remain fixed. Illustrated weathered surfaces and construction stages cover unfinished areas; completing the associated assignment removes those layers, revealing the exact same aligned clean map. No flat brown JOB rectangles, repeated code cracks, generic X crates, or volunteer clones posing as residents remain. Residents appear as relevant amenities finish rather than waiting for the whole map to be complete.
